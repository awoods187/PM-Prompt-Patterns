# Product feedback call: [Lena Ortiz, Tidewater Outdoor Co.](https://app.example-recorder.com/shared/abc123)

<!-- Fictional exemplar. People, company, product state, and links are invented. -->

## Why we lost the deal

Inventory costing is the hard blocker. Tidewater sells through Shopify and Amazon from three warehouses and needs landed cost (freight, duty, and broker fees) allocated onto each receipt so COGS is right by SKU. Today a contractor does it in a spreadsheet every month. She rejected the workaround of keeping landed cost in that spreadsheet and posting one summary journal entry, because the whole point of switching was to retire the spreadsheet.

We track inventory quantities and average cost today, but we don't support [landed cost allocation](https://linear.app/your-workspace/project/landed-cost-allocation-example) or [FIFO costing layers](https://linear.app/your-workspace/project/fifo-costing-example) yet.

They're staying on QuickBooks Enterprise through peak season. It's ugly, but inventory ties out every month and she won't risk that mid-season. We're still in consideration for a January start if landed cost lands.

## What she liked

  - She found bank rules much easier to set up than in QuickBooks, and said the rule suggestions alone would save her bookkeeper a few hours a week.
  - The AP approval flow. Their buyers place POs directly with suppliers, and routing bills to the buyer who placed the PO was exactly how she wants approvals to work. She brought it up three separate times.
  - She liked that the Shopify sync posts a daily summary instead of one entry per order.

## What felt weak

  - The demo spent twenty minutes on multi-entity, which they don't need, and never got to inventory until she asked. By then she had already decided it was a gap.
  - The trial had no sample inventory data, so she couldn't test costing at all without a full import.

## Feature wishlist

  - [Landed cost allocation](https://linear.app/your-workspace/project/landed-cost-allocation-example) by value, weight, or quantity, applied at receipt. Backlog.
  - A 3PL integration for ShipBob so warehouse transfers post automatically. No Linear project exists yet.

She also mentioned, but we already largely support:

  - Amazon settlement reconciliation. She assumed it was manual; our Amazon connector already splits settlements into sales, fees, and refunds. It never came up in the pitch.
  - Department budgets with variance alerts. We support this today, and it's worth showing when we reconnect.

## Their stack

Supported today: Shopify, Amazon Seller Central, Bill.com, and Chase (banking).

Not yet supported:

  - ShipBob (no project)
  - [Cin7](https://linear.app/your-workspace/project/cin7-integration-example)
